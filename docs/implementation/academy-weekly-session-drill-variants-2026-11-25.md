# Academy weekly session plans, timer, and linked drill proposals — 25 November 2026

Owner requirements: coaches freely select approved library drills; they may propose corrections to an existing approved drill or create a linked difficulty/level variant; central approval is necessary only for catalogue content. Coaches plan at most one week ahead. Every complete session allocates the full session duration and at least 20 minutes of game-category activity, with multiple drills and editable per-session durations. Execution needs a countdown per saved drill.

## Implemented policies and workflow

- Rolling seven calendar days including today (today through today+6, Cyprus timezone) for coach schedule creation/edit, private delivery writes, and schedule submission. Managers retain the ability to organize official calendars ahead of the coach window. Coach calendar UI displays a weekly window. No batch/recurring schedule generation in this slice.
- New independent session saves require exact full duration allocation and at least 20 aggregate minutes from approved drills whose actual catalogue category is game. Server trusts frozen catalogue category, not a client label. Up to 24 distinct drills, each 1–120 minutes, can be ordered, removed, and timed. Existing duration and version checks are retained. Session duration minimum 20 minutes. An unplanned schedule shell may exist but is labelled incomplete; cannot be submitted as a complete coach plan. Independent delivery save and coach submission both enforce the rules in SQL. Old manager-only legacy attachment writes retained for compatibility; do not count a partial legacy plan as complete. Official schedule publication stays independent of coach exercise approval. A schedule duration increase may make a previously complete sequence incomplete until reallocated.
- Coach's Greek session designer shows total allocation, game quota, and complete status. Per-exercise execution countdown starts from saved session-specific duration; start/resume, pause, reset, choose another drill and next drill. Absolute deadline handles delayed browser ticks, exact millisecond remainder on pause, stops on unmount/access rejection/edits. Timer is local browser state, not persisted or background synchronized; no notifications/audio claimed.
- New linked proposals retain exact approved source ID/version and selected beginner/intermediate/advanced difficulty, with immutable first snapshot and provenance. Variant approval creates a separate approved entry linked to source version, retaining original. Revision acceptance uses explicit Super Admin confirmation of candidate and base versions; base stays approved and untouched while pending. On acceptance, original ID receives next approved version, prior history remains intact, candidate archived with applied_version. Concurrent/stale source versions fail closed; coach self-approval and ordinary revision-candidate catalogue approval denied. Pending revision candidates excluded from central catalogue; own proposal history and pending central review queue only. Approved variants include difficulty and lineage in central catalogue and picker. Existing delivery snapshots retain the exact drill ID/version and content; explicit difficulty label in frozen historical delivery snapshots is not yet added.
- Two new API routes: POST /api/admin/academy/drills/proposals/linked and POST /api/admin/academy/drills/proposals/{drillId}/apply-revision; grant/edge/contract wiring updated; OpenAPI/TypeScript snapshots staged manually and need regenerated from compiled host.
